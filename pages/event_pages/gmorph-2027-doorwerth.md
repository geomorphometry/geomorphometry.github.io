Geomophometry Conference 2027 “The S&T of Topographic modeling and LIDAR data processing”, will be held in [Doorwerth](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doorwerth), The Netherlands, from **31st May - 03th June, 2027**. The conference is hosted by the [OpenGeoHub Foundation](https://opengeohub.org/); this is the official bi-annual meeting of the International Society of Geomorphometry. The venue will be the [Fletcher Hotel-Restaurant Doorwerth-Arnhem](https://www.hoteldoorwerth.nl/en/).  

During this four-day hybrid event, The conference aims to advance the field of geomorphometry/topographic modeling and LiDAR data processing by fostering collaboration, promoting open knowledge exchange, and supporting methodological innovation. It seeks to improve digital technologies for terrain analysis and enhance the use of elevation/relief data for applications such as environmental monitoring, hydrology, civil engineering, soil, biodiversity and vegetation mapping, land-use planning, and natural hazard assessment.

<br>
#### CALL FOR ABSTRACTS IS OPEN!

In this edition of the conference, the abstracts submission and revision will happen in **two phases**.

#### First phase - simple abstract  

**Deadline: 1st of December 2026**  

In the first pahse, you submit a proposal for an oral presentation, workshop, or poster session using the form linked below.   
All proposals will be reviewed by the Scientific Committee, and the highest-ranked proposals will be selected for inclusion in the conference programme.

#### Second phase - full 4-page paper

**Deadline: 1st of March 2027**  

If your abstract be accepted in the first phase, you will be required to submit a four-page full paper.   
This will also be reviewed by the Scientific Committee.
