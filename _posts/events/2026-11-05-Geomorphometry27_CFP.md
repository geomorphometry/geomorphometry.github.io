We are excited to announce that abstract submission is now open for the Geomorphometry Conference 2027, “The S&T of Topographic Modelling and LIDAR Data Processing.”

📅 31 May–3 June 2027
📍 Doorwerth, The Netherlands

The conference programme will feature oral presentations, workshops, poster sessions, providing opportunities to share research, exchange ideas and connect with the geomorphometry community.

📣 Interested in contributing?
Submit your abstract for an oral presentation, workshop or poster by 1 December 2026: [https://pretalx.earthmonitor.org/geomorphometry-conference-2027/cfp](https://pretalx.earthmonitor.org/geomorphometry-conference-2027/cfp)

Please note that, if your abstract is accepted, you will be required to submit a "four-page full paper" as part of the second phase of the submission process. The deadline for the full paper submission is 1 March 2027.

For more information about the conference, please visit the official conference website: [https://opengeohub.org/events/geomorphometry-conference-2027](https://opengeohub.org/events/geomorphometry-conference-2027)

🔎 This conference is hosted by the [OpenGeoHub Foundation](https://opengeohub.org/). 

👉 Submit your abstract and join us in Doorwerth in 2027!
